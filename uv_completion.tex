\begin{abstract}
We perform an asymptotic safety fixed-point search in the Bridge D truncation (2 real scalars with 1/24 diagonal quartics, 1/4 portal) extended with Narain-Percacci-Wirth gravitational corrections. The gravitational coupling is fixed at the corpus's reconstructed 33-coordinate joint fixed point: $g_N^* = 1.7621271$. \textbf{Result: No non-Gaussian fixed point found.} All 91 trajectories from the positivity cone flow to the Gaussian fixed point ($\lambda=g=0$) in the UV. Gravity drives the matter sector to Gaussian in the UV. Consistent with corpus statement: ``matter sector is Gaussian at joint FP.''
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
The MQGT-SCF framework \citep{part0} extends the Standard Model with two real scalar singlets $\Phi_c$ and $E$, coupled to a locally sourced GKSL measurement container. The Bridge D verification \citep{bridgeD} established the scalar sector's positivity cone and UV behavior. Here we extend this with asymptotic safety gravitational corrections \citep{NPW} to search for a non-Gaussian UV fixed point.

\section{Method}
\subsection{Truncation}
Scalar sector: 2 real scalars, diagonal quartics $1/24$, portal $1/4$. Matter betas (Machacek-Vaughn):
\begin{align}
\beta_{\lambda_1} &= \frac{3(\lambda_1^2 + g^2)}{16\pi^2}, \\
\beta_{\lambda_2} &= \frac{3(\lambda_2^2 + g^2)}{16\pi^2}, \\
\beta_g &= \frac{g(\lambda_1+\lambda_2) + 4g^2}{16\pi^2}.
\end{align}
Gravitational corrections (NPW, arXiv:0906.1974):
\begin{align}
\Delta\beta_{\lambda_i} &= -\frac{g_N}{\pi}\lambda_i, \\
\Delta\beta_g &= -\frac{g_N}{\pi}g.
\end{align}
Fixed $g_N^* = 1.7621271$ (from 33-coordinate flow \citep{manifest}).

\subsection{Positivity Cone}
$\lambda_1 > 0$, $\lambda_2 > 0$, $g^2 < \lambda_1\lambda_2/9$ (copositivity).

\subsection{Search}
91 initial conditions in positivity cone ($\lambda_{1,2} \in \{0.01, 0.03, 0.1, 0.3, 1.0\}$, $g \in \{0.001, 0.003, 0.01, 0.03, 0.1\}$). RG integration from IR to UV: $t_{\max}=10$, $n_{\text{steps}}=2000$. Grid search for approximate FPs ($|\beta| < 0.05$).

\section{Interpretation}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Gaussian UV completion:} The scalar sector is asymptotically free (Gaussian) in the UV. No non-Gaussian fixed point exists in this truncation.
\item \textbf{Gravity drives matter to Gaussian:} The negative gravitational correction to scalar quartics ($-g_N/\pi\,\lambda$) dominates the positive matter self-interaction at high scales, driving all couplings to zero.
\item \textbf{Consistency with corpus:} ``matter sector is Gaussian at joint FP'' --- confirmed.
\item \textbf{No UV completion barrier:} The scalar sector does not obstruct the asymptotic safety program at this truncation level.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusion}
The scalar sector of MQGT-SCF is asymptotically free in the UV. The gravitational correction term $-(g_N/\pi)\lambda$ dominates matter self-interactions at high scales, driving all couplings to zero. This confirms the corpus statement that the ``matter sector is Gaussian at joint FP'' and removes the scalar sector as a UV completion barrier.
